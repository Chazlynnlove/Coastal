# beach-party
It's a hackathon in May where you ship 65 hours to get an invite to the Beach Party Hackathon in Puerto Plata, Dominican Republic. Need based stipends are provided. The prompt is to build a game related to the beach, with hardware or software. For example, a surfing simulator, an arcade game where you avoid falling coconuts, or a beach sandbox game. You journal your progress, and the projects are reviewed. When you have 65 hours, you get emailed the invite. Then, you can have need-based or merit-based flights stipends and merit-based bus stipends. For everybody who arrives at Santiago De Los Caballeros, we get to take the bus together. The theme is announced at the hackathon.
